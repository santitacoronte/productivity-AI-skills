# Freelance Services Agreement

This Freelance Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on the date of the last signature below between **Northwind Logistics GmbH**, Hardturmstrasse 120, 8005 Zürich, Switzerland ("Client"), and **the Consultant** named in the signature block ("Consultant").

## 1. Services
1.1 Consultant shall provide process automation consulting services as described in Schedule A ("Services").
1.2 Client may request reasonable changes to the Services. Consultant shall accommodate such changes without additional charge where the change does not exceed fifteen percent (15%) of the original estimated effort.

## 2. Term
2.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months.
2.2 This Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and Payment
3.1 Client shall pay Consultant a fixed monthly fee of CHF 6,500, exclusive of VAT.
3.2 Consultant shall invoice monthly in arrears. Invoices are payable within sixty (60) days of receipt of a correct invoice.
3.3 Client may withhold payment of any invoice, in whole or in part, that it disputes in good faith until the dispute is resolved.
3.4 Expenses are reimbursed only if approved in writing by Client in advance.

## 4. Intellectual Property
4.1 All work product, deliverables, code, documentation, methods and know-how created by Consultant in connection with the Services ("Work Product") shall be the exclusive property of Client upon creation.
4.2 Consultant hereby assigns to Client all rights in the Work Product, including any pre-existing materials of Consultant incorporated into the Work Product.

## 5. Confidentiality
5.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for the term of this Agreement and five (5) years thereafter.

## 6. Non-Solicitation and Non-Competition
6.1 During the term and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Consultant shall not provide services similar to the Services to any company operating in the logistics sector in Switzerland, Germany or Austria.
6.2 During the term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither party shall solicit the employees of the other party.

## 7. Liability
7.1 Consultant's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
7.2 Client's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed CHF 1,000.
7.3 Consultant shall indemnify Client against all third-party claims arising from the Services.

## 8. Termination
8.1 Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time with fourteen (14) days' written notice.
8.2 Consultant may terminate this Agreement for convenience with ninety (90) days' written notice.
8.3 Upon termination, Client shall pay only for Services accepted by Client up to the termination date.

## 9. Independent Contractor
9.1 Consultant is an independent contractor and is responsible for all taxes and social security contributions.

## 10. Governing Law
10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Switzerland. The courts of Zürich have exclusive jurisdiction.

**Schedule A: Services**
Design and implementation of workflow automations connecting the Client's TMS, ERP and customer portal; weekly status calls; documentation; up to 20 hours per month of support.
