# Worked example: the Rotaboard deck

The deck from the Deck Doctor game (https://santiagotacoronte.com/productivity-tips/bad-presentation-examples/) and Course 1, Lesson 8.6. The company, the vendors and the numbers are made up for the exercise.

## Input

**Shift scheduling tool for Customer Support: review**

1. Title: Prepared for the leadership meeting
2. Background: the Customer Support team, 40 agents. 3 sites, 2 shifts. Shifts planned today in a shared spreadsheet.
3. Context: our three sites (Site A, Site B, Site C)
4. Methodology: how we compared scheduling tools. 14 criteria, scored 1 to 5.
5. Market overview: twelve scheduling tools
6. Features: Rotaboard (drag-and-drop rota, mobile app, shift swaps) and Teamplan (shift templates, mobile app, offline mode)
7. Pricing: Rotaboard €12 per user per month (€5,760 a year), Teamplan €15 (€7,200 a year)
8. Agent survey: 31 answers. In small print: "Two of three sites report unreliable internet. Rotaboard only works online."
9. Risks: training time, data migration
10. Conclusions: "Both tools meet our core needs. Each has strengths and trade-offs."
11. Next steps: let's discuss

## Expected diagnosis

| # | Mistake | Found? | Slide | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No decision asked | Yes | 11: "Next steps: let's discuss" | "Let's discuss" only books the next meeting. |
| 2 | No recommendation | Yes | 10: "Both tools meet our core needs" | The team knows the answer and keeps it to itself. |
| 3 | No owner and no date | Yes | Whole deck | No name and no deadline anywhere. |
| 4 | Key facts hidden | Yes | 8: "Two of three sites report unreliable internet. Rotaboard only works online." | Small print, after seven slides of background. |
| 5 | Irrelevant information | Yes | 5: "Market overview: twelve scheduling tools" | Twelve tools, for a choice between two. |
| 6 | Option missing | Yes | 2 mentions the spreadsheet, no slide offers it | Keeping today's spreadsheet is never an option. |

**6 of 6 mistakes found.**

## The two answers

1. Deciding facts: the agent survey, slide 8, and Teamplan's offline mode, slide 6.
2. Recommendation: Teamplan. Owner: the support lead. By Friday.

## Expected four slides

**1. The decision**
Approve a scheduling tool for the support team, signed by Friday.

**2. The options**
- Rotaboard: €5,760 a year. Online only.
- Teamplan: €7,200 a year. Works offline.
- Keep the spreadsheet: €0. Risk: (check), the deck doesn't say.

**3. The facts**
Two of three sites have unreliable internet, and only Teamplan works offline. Both tools have a mobile app. Teamplan costs €1,440 more a year.

**4. The recommendation**
Teamplan. It costs €1,440 more a year, and it works at all three sites. Owner: the support lead. Decision by Friday.

**Appendix:** 2 Background, 3 Context, 4 Methodology, 5 Market overview, 6 Features (offline mode now on the facts slide), 7 Pricing (now on the options slide), 8 Agent survey (full results), 9 Risks.

The winner is the more expensive tool, and the facts that decided it sat on slide 8 all along.
